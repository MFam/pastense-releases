Pastense

Clipboard history for macOS. Press ⇧⌘V, hit a number, done.

Download on the Mac App Store

Features

One shortcut

⇧⌘V opens your recent clips over whatever you're doing. Number keys paste instantly.

Preview before you paste

Long text, images, links and files show a large preview on hover.

Files and rich text

Files copied in Finder paste as files. Links and formatting from the browser survive.

Your layout

Center, left, right or a strip along the top. Keep up to 100 clips.

Private by design

History stays on your Mac. No accounts, no analytics, nothing uploaded.
